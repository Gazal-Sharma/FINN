Quarterly Performance (2Q FY27 vs 2Q FY26) 
 Gross Revenue increased by 27.8% Y-o-Y to ₹ 362,268 crore ($ 37.8 billion). 
o JPL revenue increased by 11.3% Y-o-Y driven by continued subscriber additions, ARPU increase 
and healthy growth in digital services. 
o RRVL revenue grew 6.4% Y-o-Y to ₹ 91,716 crore, with performance impacted by the festive 
calendar shift to Q3. 
o Oil to Chemicals (O2C) revenue increased by 44.2% Y-o-Y led by sharp increase in crude and 
product prices. Crude price for the quarter increased 40.6%, whereas transportation fuel prices 
were up 50-78% Y-o-Y. Prices for most of the downstream chemical products were up 25-35% 
Y-o-Y, driven by strong feedstock prices. 
o Oil and Gas segment revenue was stable Y-o-Y. KGD6 gas production as well as price realization 
was lower, which was offset by higher condensate price realization. Further, improved volumes 
and price realization for CBM supported revenues. 
 EBITDA increased by 23.9% Y-o-Y to ₹ 62,409 crore ($ 6.5 billion). 
o JPL EBITDA increased by 16.6% Y-o-Y with strong revenue growth and margin expansion of 250 
bps Y-o-Y. 
o RRVL EBITDA decreased 8.6% Y-o-Y to ₹ 6,228 crore, with margin at 7.8%, down 120 bps Y-o-
Y, reflecting planned investment phase in Digital Commerce, input-cost inflation absorbed for 
customers, and the festive shift to Q3. 
o O2C EBITDA increased by 67.9% Y-o-Y with a steep rise in transportation fuel cracks and 
downstream petrochemical margins. Fuel cracks were up 3-3.7x in a very tight global refining 
environment. Polyester chain margins improved sharply by 33%, led by 15% higher PX delta and 
52% increase in PTA delta. Earnings were constrained by higher crude oil premiums, elevated 
freight rates, SAED related costs and under-recoveries in domestic fuel retail. 
o Oil and Gas segment EBITDA was lower by 2.0% on Y-o-Y basis on account of declining KG D6 
gas revenues and higher GoI levies. This was partly offset by favourable movement in exchange 
rate.

C. STRATEGIC PROGRESS 
 
 The equity shares of the Company have been listed on the National Stock Exchange of India 
Limited (NSE) and BSE Limited (BSE) with effect from 28th October, 2026 upon completion of the 
Initial Public Offer by way of fresh issue of 27,00,00,000 equity shares of face value of ₹ 10 each 
at an issue price of ₹ 1,119 per equity share (including a securities premium of ₹ 1,109 per equity 
share) (“Issue”). The Gross Proceeds from the Issue, after accounting for employee discount of ₹ 
54 per equity share, aggregated ₹ 30,188 crore. 
 An aggregate amount of ₹ 27,500 crore from the Net Proceeds has been utilized towards 
prepayment, in full or in part, of the principal amount outstanding of certain External Commercial 
Borrowings availed by Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited, material subsidiary of the Company and 
the balance amount will be utilised towards general corporate purposes, in line with the Objects 
of the Issue as set out in the Prospectus. 
 Jio extended its leadership in 5G subscriber base with 300 million users as of September 2026, 
up 66 million over the last 12 months. Customer engagement also remained robust with 5G now 
accounting for ~56% of total wireless traffic. 
 Total Fixed Broadband subscribers as of September 2026 stood at 30.0 million with 7.3 million 
additions over the last 12 months. Jio AirFiber with total base of 15.1 million subscribers, has 
driven more than 75% of Jio’s fixed broadband additions during the last 12 months. 
 During the India Mobile Congress 2026, Jio Platforms showcased Jio Call Agent which using AI 
can transcribe and summarise calls for every participant and can act as a concierge. This can be 
invoked during an ongoing call without downloading an application or adding an additional number. 
Jio also unveiled JioFrames, AI-native smart camera frames featuring a discreet built-in camera, 
voice assistance (‘Hello Jio’), hands-free calling, live translation across 12 languages, and UPI 
payments.  
 JioGames has continued to enhance its catalogue with new additions like Final Fantasy VII, 
Cricket26, War Thunder, and Mafia II during the quarter. All these premium titles and more are 
available on JioGames Pro Pass with subscription plans starting at ₹ 48.

sectors.

RESULTS CONTEXT
Trent Limited – Investor Presentation Q2FY27
❖ Market context: 
─ Our concepts registered healthy traction, especially seen in the context of the shift of the festive window this year. The consumer sentiment 
was broadly positive and resonated well across our product offerings, brands and categories. 
─ Structurally the outlook for organised fashion retail remains encouraging, supported by formalisation, higher consumer aspirations, and 
increased consumption propensity. Our experience in non-Metro / Tier 1 towns also resonates with these trends.
─ The inflation levels across line items remains elevated. The volatile geopolitical circumstances, interest rate trends and supply-chain 
disruptions continue to contribute to this playout. Our endeavour has been to maintain relative stability of the value proposition for our 
customers. Our sourcing actions and broader supplier engagements continue to help us navigate these inflationary headwinds.
❖ Growth agenda: 
─ During the quarter we opened the 1000th Zudio Store. We now operate a significant portfolio of over 1300 “large-box” fashion stores, with 
presence across 339 cities (including 3 in the UAE). In Q2FY27 we opened 10 Westside and 19 Zudio stores and consolidated 2 Zudio stores.  As 
of 30th September 2026, our store portfolio included 311 Westside, 999 Zudio (including 7 stores in the UAE) and 32 stores across other 
lifestyle concepts and we operated with a footprint of over 18.5 million sqft. across our fashion brands. 
─ On an annual basis we expect the trajectory of new store openings to remain broadly consistent. Quarterly store openings are inherently 
asymmetric given the complexities around property development, fitout timelines & regulatory approvals. 
─ Our store portfolio remains heterogeneous across markets and maturity cohorts. We continue to deepen our presence in select existing 
catchments while expanding into secondary cities and emerging micro-markets. Approximately 85% of Zudio stores opened during the quarter 
were in peripheral growth markets or in Tier II cities & beyond. 
─ Greater cluster density, coupled with an improving customer proposition, is delivering encouraging revenue throughput and profitability 
outcomes. Newer and secondary markets offer attractive growth potential despite relatively longer maturity profile and hence, these stores are 
not entirely comparable with those of the established portfolio.

RESULTS CONTEXT
Trent Limited – Investor Presentation Q2FY27
❖ Growth agenda (contd.) :
─ In managing our store network, we consistently seek to optimise the quality of brand presence in micro-markets and the economic 
attractiveness of the portfolio in terms of profitability, return on invested capital & operating cashflows.
─ Like-for-like growth for our fashion portfolio in Q2FY27 was in the mid-single digits. The emerging categories, including beauty & personal care, 
innerwear and footwear contributed over 21% of our revenues. Westside online together with our proposition on the Tata Neu platform 
continues to witness interesting traction and grow profitably. In H1FY27, online revenues grew by 20% and contributed to over 6% of Westside 
revenues.
─ Our newer brands (Burnt Toast and Samoh), emerging categories and new geographies including Zudio in the Middle East market are at varying 
stages of evolution. We are generally encouraged by the positive traction, improving viability and the exciting growth possibilities afforded by 
these initiatives. 
─ Competitive context: Given the size and attractiveness of the fashion/ lifestyle market in India, the competitive intensity has been high over the 
past few years, with no material recent shift. The competitive landscape remains asymmetrical due to varying scale, size and distribution 
channels across brands and players. Our model involving a portfolio of own brands and direct-to-consumer distribution, affords us the ability to 
integrally differentiate the customer value proposition. We remain focused on strengthening our core to drive efficiencies, enhancing our 
product proposition, on-trend fashion and driving premiumisation by way of desirable products, convenience and predictable experience. Over 
time, we seek to build upon and deepen our competitive moat by growing the relevance of our brands across target audiences and all markets.  
─ Star, our food and grocery business, has been witnessing encouraging consumer traction notwithstanding the evolving competitive landscape. 
Star revenues grew by over 20% during the quarter. The sales mix continues to favour own brands and increasingly delivers a viable economic 
profile at the store level. We added 1 new store during the quarter across the Star portfolio taking the overall store count to 87 across 13 cities. 
We remain excited about the potential of this business.

RESULTS CONTEXT

❖ Operating economics: 
─ Other income for the previous year includes pre-tax profit on sale of assets of Rs. 96 cr in Q2FY26 and Rs. 109 cr in H1FY26. The corresponding 
profit on sale of assets in Q2 and H1FY27 is not material. This being a non-operating profit element, nevertheless, reflects in the PAT numbers of 
Q2 and H1FY26. 
─ Product elevation and premiumization across select categories enables us to broaden our offerings and expand addressable markets. We 
believe a broadly stable price architecture across categories, a consistent focus on driving efficiencies and maintaining a relatively stable 
margin profile over time would yield better salience for our brands.
─ Focus on structural cost optimization together with realising operating efficiencies is integral to our strategy. We are experiencing encouraging 
economic outcomes from actively managing our existing store portfolio through customer experience upgrades and improving brand salience. 
This continues to be a significant agenda and a key lever for delivering consistent performance.
─ Across the business, we are progressively deploying AI tools and analytical models that seek to amplify our performance. Our digital, 
technological and AI driven interventions are key to facilitate optimal management of growing scale and driving productivity. Multiple of these 
initiatives are starting to yield encouraging results across a spectrum of objectives including revenue, cost and capital optimisation. Over the 
near to medium term, we are accelerating our commitment to forward deployment and integration of several AI/digital initiatives.
─ We continue to substantially invest our internal accruals to support growth and the resilience of our brands. These commitments, including 
capital expenditure, cover opening of new stores, refresh of select stores from the existing portfolio, expanding warehousing and supply chain 
infrastructure along with a range of automation and technology related investments. Our capex outflows are materially related to the foregoing 
agenda.
─ We prefer to retain operating control of our stores and the ownership of merchandise at all times. Our approach to franchising has been 
selective given diverse objectives and primarily related to properties. Our preferred operating model is for stores to be Company-Operated.

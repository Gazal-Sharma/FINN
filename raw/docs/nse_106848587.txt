* Pursuant to the Rights Issue ("Issue") undertaken by the Bharti Airtel Limited (the "Company") in terms of the Letter of 
Offer dated September 22, 2021 ("Letter of Offer'J, the Company had issued and allotted 392,287,662 equity shares, to 
its eligible shareholders at an Issue Price of Rs. 535 per share (including a premium of Rs. 530), in the ratio of 1 (One) 
additional equity share for every 14 (Fourteen) equity shares held by eligible shareholders as on the record date i.e. 
September 28, 2021. Accordingly, the aggregate Issue size amounted to Rs. 20,987.39 Crore. 
In accordance with the terms of the Issue as specified in the Letter of Offer, the Company received Rs. 133. 75 per share 
(comprising Rs. 1.25 towards face value and Rs. 132.50 towards premium) as application money, and consequently, the 
partly paid-up equity shares were allotted by the Company on October 27, 2021. 
The Board of Directors ("Board'? of the Company, in its meeting held on December 18, 2025, had decided to make the 
First and Final Call of Rs. 401.25 per share (comprising Rs. 3. 75 towards face value and Rs. 397.50 towards premium)

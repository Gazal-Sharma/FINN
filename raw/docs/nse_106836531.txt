building strong partnerships and significantly contributing to the company’s 
success. Mr. Prithvi is committed to positioning Dixon Technologies as an 
engineering powerhouse. He is leading efforts to diversify into new product

Scheme to the extent that its eligible revenues exceed the prescribed annual ceiling limit. Recognition of such incremental incentive income is permitted under
the Scheme in circumstances where other eligible applicants within the same target segment have not fully utilised their respective ceiling entitlements. The
recognition is based on management's assessment of the performance of other applicants, using publicly available information and internally available data.

receivable as at 30 September 2026, pending formal determination and disbursement by the Project Management Agency ("PMA"). Correspondingly, the
subsidiary company has recognised a liability payable to its customer as per the arrangement amounting to Rs. 576.95 crores in respect of the same incentive.

actively pursue receipt of the incentive for the relevant performance years through ongoing engagement With the PMA and other concerned authorities.

subsidiary's eligibility for incentive upon achievement of the prescribed conditions under the Scheme.
